Privacy Policy

Last updated:

5 sept. 2025

At Slice Town, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you interact with our website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you visit our site or place an order:

  • Personal Information: Name, email, phone number, and delivery address (only when you submit a form or place an order).

  • Payment Information: If applicable, processed securely via third-party services (we do not store payment details).

  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, time spent on pages, and other analytics to improve site performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process orders and deliver your pizza.

  • Respond to your questions and feedback.

  • Improve the design and performance of our website.

  • Send updates or promotions (only if you opt-in).

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share information with trusted service providers to:

  • Host and maintain the website.

  • Process payments (e.g., Stripe, PayPal).

  • Analyze website traffic (e.g., Google Analytics).

4. Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Remember your preferences.

  • Analyze site traffic and improve user experience.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

5. Data Security

We take appropriate steps to protect your data through secure hosting, SSL encryption, and limited access controls.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access or update your personal data.

  • Request deletion of your information.

  • Withdraw consent at any time.

Contact us at info@email.com to make a request.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to other sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices and encourage you to read their policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. The latest version will always be posted here with the updated date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this policy, reach out to us.
