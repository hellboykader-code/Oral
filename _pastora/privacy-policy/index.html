Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit or interact with our restaurant.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and reservation details when you:

Make a booking

Place an order

Subscribe to our newsletter

Contact us directly

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information helps us:

Manage reservations and orders efficiently

Improve our website and services

Send updates, offers, or event invitations (only if you opt in)

Respond to your inquiries and feedback

3. Sharing Your Information

We respect your privacy. Your personal data is never sold or traded.   We may share limited information with trusted partners (such as payment or reservation systems) only when necessary to provide our services.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies to:

Enhance your browsing experience

Remember your preferences

Analyze website traffic

You can choose to disable cookies in your browser settings.

5. Data Security

We take appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

Access or update your personal information

Request deletion of your data

Opt out of marketing communications at any time

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, feel free to contact us:
